What Rhythm requires

  • An iPhone running iOS 26 or later
  • A paired Rhythm dock
  • Alarm permission
  • Screen Time permission for app and website blocking

How do I stop the alarm?

Walk to your dock and hold the top of your iPhone on it. The system Stop button only silences the current ring; Rhythm schedules another ring until you dock or the safety limit is reached.

Does Rhythm need to remain open overnight?

No. Lock your iPhone normally. Rhythm uses Apple’s system alarm framework.

Why is nothing blocked after I dock?

Open Rhythm, go to Settings, then Permissions. Confirm Screen Time access is active and confirm that you selected apps or websites before the alarm.

What if my dock is lost or broken?

Remove it under Settings, Docks. Alarms remain disabled until another dock is paired.

Can I use more than one dock?

Yes. Any paired dock can complete the alarm sequence.
