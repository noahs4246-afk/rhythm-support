Privacy Policy

Rhythm is designed to work without an account and without sending your app activity to us.

Information Rhythm stores

Rhythm stores the information needed to provide its features on your iPhone and in an Apple App Group shared with its Screen Time extension. This includes alarm schedules and preferences, identifiers of docks you pair, focus-block duration and status, saved Focus modes and session rules, Focus-session start and end times, successful alarm dock-scan timestamps shown in Activity, Emergency Unlock usage dates, Apple’s opaque Screen Time selection data, and onboarding and permission-status settings.

This information remains on your device. Rhythm does not operate a server that receives it.

Resetting Rhythm removes alarms, paired docks, modes, and activity history. Emergency Unlock usage is retained locally so resetting preferences does not renew the monthly allowance.

NFC

When you pair or scan a Rhythm dock, the app reads the NFC tag identifier so it can recognize that dock later. The identifier is stored locally on your device and is not sent to us.

Screen Time

Rhythm uses Apple’s Family Controls, Managed Settings, and Device Activity frameworks to let you select and temporarily shield apps and websites. Apple provides the app with opaque selection values. Rhythm does not transmit your selections to us.

Data collection and tracking

Rhythm does not include advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking SDKs. We do not collect personal information through the app and do not track you across apps or websites.

Support email

If you contact us by email, we receive the email address and information you choose to send. We use that information only to respond to your request and maintain support records. Your email provider and ours may process the message under their own privacy policies.

Children

Rhythm does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Rhythm changes. The effective date above identifies the current version.
